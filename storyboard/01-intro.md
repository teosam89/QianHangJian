## Part 01 · Intro · f0–f360

Chibi 千行 cuts your black screen open on the song's first sound (f8–f9); it falls away on the bar-0 hit (f14), onto the
red city (R 1.00). She drops through red error signboards, lands on a ridge (f121), glances back at us, and her HUD
locks five bugs and the hackers' hanging signboard. On the drums' entry (f194) her cut carves 「千行剑」 into the
signboard and her next cut stamps 「赛博江湖」 on it (f205). She runs the roofs killing a bug per snare, and Enter combo
#1 (f329–f351) cuts 千·行·剑; on the release (f357) she already sits on the last ridge, looking up at the moon, as the
banner bursts into rain.
